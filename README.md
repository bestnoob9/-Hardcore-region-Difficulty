### ⚔️ Hardcore Region Difficulty

- Where you stand decides how dangerous the world is.
- Hardcore Region Difficulty is a location-based difficulty mod for **Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge)**. Monster strength depends on the dimension, biome and structure you are in, never on your progression. Go deeper into dangerous places, and you will face tougher mobs, but also find better gear and rarer enchantments.
- Inspired by Dungeon Difficulty and Quality Equipment.

---
---
 🔥 Features
### ☠️ Regional Difficulty Scaling
- Biome, dimension & structure based: every region has a difficulty level (1-5). Structures like Nether Fortresses, Ancient Cities or End Cities raise it.
- Smart Auto mode: automatically rates dangerous areas based on nearby spawners and clusters of hostile mobs.
- Stronger hostile mobs: health, damage, armor and speed grow with each difficulty level. Bosses are left untouched.
- On-screen region titles announce the danger level when you enter a new area, plus boss proximity warnings.
- Fully configurable with flexible patterns: exact IDs, `#tags`, `~regex`, `*` and `!` negation.

### 🗡️ Item Quality System
- Weapons, tools, armor, ranged weapons and shields can have a quality, from negative ones (Rusted, Damaged) up to Flawless.
- Quality Forge: a new block to reroll and upgrade your equipment's quality using materials.
- Item tooltips show the exact stat changes.

✨ Advanced Enchantments
10 powerful enchantments that go beyond vanilla limits: Advanced Protection, Sharpness, Power, Fire Aspect, Thorns, Feather Falling, Looting, Efficiency, Unbreaking and Knockback, plus True Damage, Armor Piercing and Swift Strike.
- Found in chests of dangerous structures, and as librarian trades (expensive and limited).
- Never from the enchanting table, fishing or mob drops.
- The higher the region difficulty, the higher the enchant levels you can find.

### 🌌 Optional Compat: Deeper and Darker × Cataclysm
Enable `deeperdarker_cataclysm` in the config to link two of the hardest mods:
- Cataclysm's Ignitium and Cursium armor are now crafted from Deeper and Darker Warden gear instead of Netherite.
- Requires both Deeper and Darker and L_Ender's Cataclysm when the option is on.

🛠️ Commands

| Command | Permission | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `/hrd region` | Everyone | Show the region and difficulty where you stand |
| `/hrd reload` | OP | Reload the config (an invalid file keeps the old config) |
| `/hrd scan` | OP | Rescan structures manually |
Config file: `config/hardcore_region_difficulty.json`

## 📋 Requirements & Dependencies

* **Minecraft 1.20.1**
* **Forge 47.4.20+** (Fabric and NeoForge are not supported)
* *Optional:* Deeper and Darker + Cataclysm for the compat feature
* **[Cloth Config API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cloth-config)** *(Required for in-game configuration screen)*
